# Installing the tracker on a phone

The tracker is a web app that installs from the browser; there is nothing in the App Store or Play Store. Once installed it has its own icon and opens full-screen like any other app, and it stays signed in.

Address until go-live: https://ignite-tracker.pages.dev (session 6 moves it to tracker.igniteglass.com; the install steps are the same).

## iPhone (Safari)

1. Open **Safari** (it must be Safari; Chrome on iPhone cannot add to the home screen).
2. Go to the address above and sign in.
3. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen).
4. Scroll the list and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
5. Leave the name as **Ignite** and tap **Add** (top right).
6. Close Safari. The orange flame icon is on the home screen; tap it. It opens full-screen with no address bar.

Requires iOS 16.4 or later for push reminders (session 7); installing works on older versions too.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open **Chrome**.
2. Go to the address above and sign in.
3. Chrome usually shows an **Install** banner at the bottom; tap **Install**. If there is no banner, tap the **⋮** menu (top right) and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**, then **Install**.
4. The orange flame icon appears on the home screen (and in the app drawer); tap it.

## Checking it worked

- The app opens with no browser address bar.
- It opens straight to **My Work** without asking for a password again.
- An order's **Add today's entry** button, a few taps on the chips, and **Save entry** should take under a minute.

## If something is off

- **Asks to sign in again:** sign in once; the installed app keeps the session afterwards.
- **Shows an old version:** close the app fully and open it again; updates download in the background and apply on the next open.
- **Icon missing on iPhone:** the Share → Add to Home Screen step was done in Chrome instead of Safari; repeat it in Safari.
